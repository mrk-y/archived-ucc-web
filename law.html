COLLEGE OF LAW

ABOUT THE COLLEGE

“

VISION

A quality higher legal educational institution that strives for distinction imbued with relevant knowledge, skills and values and contributes to the community by motivating the legal professionals in and for the service of the people.

“

MISSION

To develop future legal professionals to excel academically and ethically in their professional and personal lives by being driven, socially sensitive and community aware.

“

GOALS

To promote excellence in legal education and develop responsible legal professionals who are prepared for leadership, sustainable development of the law, and the administration of justice from the city to the country.

“

OBJECTIVES

  1. Develop future legal professionals with relevant legal knowledge, skills, and values.
  2. Promote academic excellence and ethical conduct in the study and practice of law.
  3. Develop socially sensitive and community-aware legal professionals committed to service.
  4. Prepare graduates for responsible leadership and meaningful participation in the administration of justice.
“

HISTORY

The UCC College of Law is a brainchild of Mayor Oscar G. Malapitan. It was established in the pursuit of his “Tao and Una” philosophy of governance and his vision for the city that firmly upholds justice and the rule of law.

With this vision to make quality legal education accessible to the poor, the UCC College of Law was conceptualized in 2014 and formally created through Sangguniang Panglunsod Ordinance No. 0569, s. 2015, dated 10 March 2015 and approved by Hon. Malapitan on 19 March 2015.

Conferring a degree in Bachelor of Laws (LIB), the College aims to promote excellence and superiority in providing legal education for responsible leadership, sustainable development of the law and the administration of justice from the city to the country.
