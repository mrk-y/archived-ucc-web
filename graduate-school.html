GRADUATE SCHOOL

ABOUT THE GRADUATE SCHOOL

“

VISION

A high quality of learning that will bring forth a high quality of life, more particularly in Caloocan City.

“

MISSION

To maintain and support an adequate system of tertiary education that will help promote economic growth of the country, strengthen the character and well-being of its graduates as productive members of the community.

“

PHILOSOPHY

Education is the essential factor to one’s personal advancement and an integral toll to nation development.

PROGRAMS

Explore the graduate programs offered by the University of Caloocan City Graduate School.

01

Doctor in Public Administration

The University of Caloocan City Doctor in Public Administration Program is a comprehensive course that provides an advanced and global approach in theoretical, critical, practical, and actual integration and application of research, knowledge, skills, capabilities, and strategies in the performance of governance and public service.

Read more →
02

Doctor of Philosophy, Major in Educational Management

The program aims to produce graduates who are competent and intellectually, socially, spiritually, and morally prepared to meet the educational and information needs of a multicultural community. It also develops moral character, personal integrity, family solidarity, citizenship, unity, and peace.

Read more →
03

Master in Public Administration

The University of Caloocan City Master in Public Administration Program is an extensive course designed to provide students with high-quality education in preparation for careers or career advancement in government and the public sector, with emphasis on professionalism, good governance, research, knowledge, ability, and skills.

Read more →
04

Master of Arts in Education, Major in Educational Management

The program aims to update and enrich teachers’ content knowledge, enhance and expand theoretical and technical knowledge for teaching, and develop teachers’ research capabilities for reviewing, verifying, validating, contextualizing, and applying theoretical and practical knowledge.

Read more →
05

Master of Arts in Education, Major in Teaching in the Early Grades

The program aims to update and enrich teachers’ content knowledge, enhance and expand their theoretical and technical knowledge for teaching, and develop their research capabilities for reviewing, verifying, validating, contextualizing, and applying theoretical and practical knowledge.

Read more →
06

Master of Arts in Education, Major in Teaching Science

The program aims to update and enrich teachers’ content knowledge, enhance and expand their theoretical and technical knowledge for teaching, and develop teachers’ research capabilities for reviewing, verifying, validating, contextualizing, and applying theoretical and practical knowledge in education.

Read more →
07

Master of Business Administration

The program develops entrepreneurial skills and leadership capability to address the nature and complexity of today’s diversified domestic and global business environment. It provides in-depth understanding of core business functions through critical and analytical skills while promoting collaboration, communication, and flexibility.

Read more →
08

Master of Science in Criminal Justice, Major in Criminology

The program prepares students to excel in the field of Criminology, equips them with advanced knowledge and skills necessary for professional practice, encourages advanced and independent research in criminology, and trains professionals to become globally competitive in the field.
